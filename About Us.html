About Hearth Remote

Android · Smart TV Remote Control

Hearth Remote lets you control your smart TVs and compatible devices from your Android phone with a clean and modern interface.

Features

  • Discover and connect to nearby smart TVs
  • Full remote control (power, volume, navigation, media, numbers)
  • Multiple device management
  • Haptic feedback and customization options
